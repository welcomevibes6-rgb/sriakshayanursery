OUR STORY

Growing With Kadiyam's Green Heritage

Sri Akshaya Nursery is rooted in Kadiyam, Andhra Pradesh — one of India's best-known horticulture and nursery hubs. With 30+ years of experience in horticulture, we have grown with a simple purpose: to supply healthy, quality plants and create greener spaces for homes, businesses and landscapes.

From beautiful ornamental plants and flowering varieties to fruit plants, avenue trees, big trees and landscaping greenery, we offer a wide range of plants carefully grown and supplied with attention to quality.

“Every healthy plant begins with good care, good soil and a little patience.”
